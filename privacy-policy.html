Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 28, 2026 · Applies to Kromar (iOS & Android)

1. 100% On-Device Processing — Zero Data Collection

Kromar is an offline physical spectral film darkroom. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share your photos, EXIF metadata, editing parameters, or personal information.

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No cloud uploads and no remote image processing.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising identifiers.
  • Editing records and imported copies stay in the app's private storage on your device.

2. Photo Library & File Access

Kromar reads only the photos or camera RAW files you explicitly pick through the system photo picker or system document picker. It does not request broad photo-library permissions, and exported images are written only to the destination you choose.

3. In-App Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (kromar_pro_lifetime) is processed entirely by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. Kromar never sees or stores your payment details; it only keeps your verified unlock state locally so offline development keeps working.

4. Network Use

Kromar touches the network only for platform billing (Google Play or App Store) and when you tap a link that opens in your browser — source code, open-source licenses, or this policy. Photos, EXIF metadata, and editing parameters are never transmitted.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: qing.pek@gmail.com.
